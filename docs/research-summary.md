# Research & Open-Source Code Lineage

Every optimization round in `docs/40round-ledger.json` was grounded in published research and open-source GPU inference codebases, adapted from scratch for AMD RDNA4 (`gfx1201`, wave32, 64 CUs, 640 GB/s, Windows WDDM):

- **Prefix Caching & Disk State:** [SGLang RadixAttention / SLRU](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/main/python/sglang/srt/mem_cache/radix_cache.py) ([arXiv:2312.07104](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.07104)), [LMCache](https://github.com/LMCache/LMCache), [Unified Radix Cache](https://www.sglang.io/blog/unified-radix-cache)
- **Loop-Controller & MoE Kernel Fusions:** [vLLM Inkling-2-Mini](https://vllm-project.github.io/2026/11/20/inkling2-mini.html), [SGLang Inkling-2-Mini](https://www.sglang.io/blog/inkling-2-small-day0-support#performance-and-optimizations), [ROCm/aiter](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter), [Dao-AILab/causal-conv1d](https://github.com/Dao-AILab/causal-conv1d)
- **RDNA4 (`gfx1201`) WMMA & MoE Scheduling:** [ROCm/composable_kernel](https://github.com/ROCm/composable_kernel), [AMD RDNA4 WMMA GEMM Blog](https://rocm.blogs.amd.com/software-tools-optimization/rdna-fp8-gemm/README.html), [ggml-org/llama.cpp PR #32065](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/32065), [SonicMoE / ScatterMoE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14080), [ExLlamaV3](https://github.com/turboderp-org/exllamav3)
- **Long-Context & Incremental Attention:** [ROCm/aiter `fmha_gfx1201`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ROCm/aiter/main/aiter/ops/flydsl/kernels/fmha_gfx1201/flash_attn_func.py), [FlashInfer](https://github.com/flashinfer-ai/flashinfer) ([arXiv:2501.01005](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01005))
- **Speculative Decoding, Shortlists & Lookup:** [DSpark](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05147v1), [Leviathan Speculative Sampling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.17192), [FR-Spec](https://github.com/thunlp/FR-Spec) ([arXiv:2502.14856](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14856)), [MicroSpec](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26444), [SAM Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10666), [ForeSpark](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.13442v1), [EAGLE-2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16858)
- **GPU Sampling & Runtime:** [FlashInfer Top-K](https://github.com/flashinfer-ai/flashinfer), [llama.cpp PR #9702](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/9702), [ROCm/clr](https://github.com/ROCm/clr), [Mirage MPK](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22219)
